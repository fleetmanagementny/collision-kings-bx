# Collision Kings BX — premium GitHub Pages website

## Upload
Upload `index.html`, `CNAME`, and the entire `assets/` folder to the **root** of your existing `fleetmanagementny/collision-kings-bx` GitHub repository, replacing `index.html`. Do not upload the enclosing folder as a nested directory. GitHub Pages remains set to **main / (root)**. Do not change your GoDaddy DNS records.

## Before using the site for real customers
1. In `index.html`, replace `REPLACE_WITH_SHOP_EMAIL` with your real shop email. The estimate form uses **mailto**, not an automated backend.
2. Replace Google Maps search URLs with your exact verified Google Business Profile review and directions links. The search links are functional fallbacks but do not guarantee a specific listing. To add a direct **Write a Review** link, get it from your Google Business Profile. No live review feed is included.
3. Add your confirmed phone number, street address, and hours once verified. These were intentionally not invented.
4. The ADAS hero and service art are **original stylized SVG illustrations**, not photos of your shop, proof of ADAS calibration services, or customer vehicles. The actual shop exterior image is in `assets/shop-front.jpg`. If you want photographs for every service, replace the SVG images with photos you own or have permission to use and update their paths.
5. Test the form and links before publicizing the site.
6. HTTPS becomes available after GitHub completes DNS validation and certificate issuance.

All files are static, mobile-friendly, and self-contained; no frameworks, external fonts, tracking scripts, or paid hosting required.
