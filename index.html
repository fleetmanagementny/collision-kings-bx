About Collision Kings

Real People. Serious Attention to Detail.

Collision Kings of USA Corp. is a Bronx-based vehicle repair business serving individual drivers and commercial operators. We bring together body repair, refinishing, structural work, suspension, and select mechanical services to help simplify the repair experience.

We know an accident can interrupt your routine or your business. Our goal is to make the next steps clearer—from reviewing the damage and discussing repair options to coordinating repairs and helping with insurance-related documentation.

Whether it's a daily driver, luxury vehicle, or a working fleet unit, we focus on clear communication, careful workmanship, and practical solutions.
